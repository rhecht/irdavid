Preservation notes

These are independent HTML5 reimplementations, not exact conversions of the original ActionScript. The original archive contains MatchingPairs2.swf, games_tetris.swf and ninja.swf; recovered bitmap assets from all three are used here. Scores are local to your browser session; the original external high-score service is not used.
