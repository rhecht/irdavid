# Opening sequence and Hebrew classic shell — screenshot-guided revision

Based on the eleven screenshots provided October 15, 2026 plus the prior archived walkthrough.

## Changes
- `index.html` now reproduces the three opening screens (logo on black, night photo and title, black-and-white language chooser). `SKIP` shows language chooser immediately; Escape also works; Replay starts it again. The original opening logo and photograph backgrounds were recovered from the SWF assets, not drawn from scratch.
- `hebrew.html` is a screenshot-matched Hebrew section-page sample (the surviving “מן המקורות” view), with a compact 765px header, original bilingual logo, archaeological backdrops, 2010-style navigation/dropdowns, left sidebar, and the surviving Hebrew “מן המקורות” text references alongside the original `mekorot4.jpg` illustration.
- `sources.html` provides native HTML5 anchors for entries shown in the screenshot. The screenshot-supported headings and references are included; uncaptured full passages have not been invented.
- `classic-article.html` and `hebrew-archive.html` provide working navigation destinations and identify other recoverable HTML pages.
- Previous English restoration and all interactive games remain available from `explore.html`; all pre-existing sections, CSS, and media remain intact.

## Accuracy
The old masthead and specific image content were matched to archived files. The original full-width strip image from the archive is only 3 pixels wide and cannot reproduce the original background photograph, so recovered archaeological photographs and the original `bgPic1.jpg` are used to approximate its background. Timed opening transitions are an HTML5 interpretation; exact Flash timing is not verified. Unsupported languages are clearly labeled.

## Run
Open `index.html` in Chrome, Firefox, or Safari. Use the language picker. No Flash, ASP, server-side runtime, network services, or libraries are needed.

## What still needs work
- The original post-intro Flash hub and the Hebrew homepage contain scripted elements for which screenshots/assets are incomplete. The current `hebrew.html` is a faithful section-page *reference*, not a claim that the entire original homepage has been recovered.
- Some original Flash menu animations and content links are represented by modern dropdowns, not exact ActionScript reproductions.
- Full Hebrew article bodies outside the supplied HTML have not been invented.
