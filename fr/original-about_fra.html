City of David

Welcome to where it all began… The story of the City of David begins over 3,000 years ago when King David left the city of Hebron for a small hilltop settlement known as Jerusalem, to establish the unified capital of the tribes of Israel. Years later, David’s son, King Solomon, built the First Temple near the City of David on Mount Moriah, the site of Isaac’s sacrifice; thanks to this Temple, the hill became one of the most important places in the world. Today, the story of the City of David continues. Deep underground, the City of David reveals fascinating archaeological remains of the ancient world. At the same time, above ground, the City is a vibrant place of activity with a visitor center that welcomes tourists to explore the site where much of the Bible was written. The City of David tour begins at the captivating observation point of biblical Jerusalem, which transports visitors back 3,800 years to the time of Abraham, the city's founding period. The tour continues underground to the site of the most recent archaeological excavations. Here, while visiting newly discovered fortresses and passageways, visitors relive David's conquest of the Jebusite city, as described in the Second Book of Samuel. The underground tour concludes at the Gihon Spring, Jerusalem's main water source for over 1,000 years, and the site where, according to the Book of Kings, King Solomon was anointed. Adventurous visitors can bring their flashlights and walk to the spring through the Tunnel of Jezekiah, a marvel of ancient engineering, excavated nearly 2,700 years ago. The City of David tour allows visitors to see the figures and places of the Bible firsthand. Indeed, this is the only place on earth where the only tour guide needed is the Bible itself. We await you in the City of David.
